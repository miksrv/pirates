# Progression & Economy (planned)

Not implemented. Design doc for XP, levels, coins, and gambling mechanics.

## Experience & Levels

**Core rule:** levels NEVER give combat advantage. Level = status + cosmetics + content unlock.

### Level unlocks
- Lv 1-5: basic ship color skins
- Lv 5: ranked mode access
- Lv 8: custom flag emblem
- Lv 10: title next to name ("Captain", "Corsair"...)
- Lv 15: water trail effects
- Lv 20: cannonball color/effects
- Lv 25+: rare frames, titles

### Level also affects
- Matchmaking brackets (anti-stomp)
- Mode gating (ranked/CTF require min level — anti-smurf)
- Perk slots: currently 1 perk; unlock 2nd at Lv 5, 3rd at Lv 10

### XP sources
- Kill: +20 XP
- Assist: +10 XP
- Win round: +50 XP
- Survive full round: +15 XP
- Daily first win: +100 XP bonus

## Coins (economy)

### Earning
- Kill: 10-20 coins
- Round win: 50
- MVP bonus: +30
- Daily challenges: 100-200
- Win streak multiplier: x1.5, x2

### Spending

**Cosmetics (primary sink):**
- Ship skins: 200-1000
- Sails (color/pattern): 150-500
- Figurehead: 300-800
- Sinking effect: 500-1500
- Water trails: 200-600

**Functional (not pay-to-win):**
- Unlock new perks: 500 coins (sidegrades, not upgrades)
- Wager match entry fees
- Tournament entry fees

## Gambling / Excitement Mechanics

### 1. Wager Match
- Bet 50/100/200 coins before match
- Winner takes the pot (all bets pooled)
- Or top-3 split: 60%/25%/15%
- Lose = lose stake

### 2. Bounty Board
- Place a bounty on any player (50+ coins)
- Whoever kills them claims the reward
- Target sees: "A bounty of 150🪙 is on your head!"
- Creates drama + targeted hunting

### 3. Treasure Chest Gamble (lobby loot boxes)
- Bronze (100), Silver (300), Gold (800 coins)
- Random prize: rare skin, coins x0.5-x5, or junk
- Drop rates shown transparently

### 4. Double or Nothing (post-match)
- After winning: "Bet your earnings on next match? x2 or 0"
- Can stack up to x8; one loss = everything gone
- Classic "one more game" hook

### 5. Liar's Dice (lobby mini-game)
- While waiting for match — quick dice game with other queued players
- Coin stakes, 30-sec rounds
- Pirate-thematic (Pirates of the Caribbean)

### 6. Daily High-Stakes Mode
- Once per day: 200 coin entry, winner takes entire pool
- 10 players × 200 = 2000 to winner
- 1/day limit creates FOMO + daily ritual
